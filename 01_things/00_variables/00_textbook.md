# Variables

Computer programs keep track of data over time.
All programming languages have a notion of a &variable; that keeps a piece of data.
A variable has three conceptual parts.
A &variable-name; is the short word or words that appear in the computer program.
The &variable-type; is a conceptual tracking of what kinds of data are allowed in the variable.
And the &variable-value; is the specific data that is in the variable while the program is running.
